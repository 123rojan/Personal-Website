About

I'm an electrical engineer from Bhaktapur, Nepal. I hold a Bachelor's in Electrical Engineering from Khwopa College of Engineering, Tribhuvan University, where my thesis on current harmonic-induced heating and transformer ageing received the Outstanding Paper Award at RESSD-2025.

My research sits where power electronics meets advanced control. I'm currently Co-PI on a grant developing model predictive control for the high-frequency converters inside solid-state transformers, and I have published on rooftop PV penetration and feeder reliability in Electric Power Systems Research.

I also teach. As a part-time Lecturer I taught Control Systems (ENEE 251) and ran labs in control, DSAP and data communication. I've delivered MATLAB, LaTeX and AutoCAD training to junior students and serve on the technical committee of the Journal of Science and Engineering.
